# Raven Labs — Tone of Voice

- Direct and authoritative, not hypey. No "revolutionary", "game-changing",
  "unlock", "supercharge".
- Speak to the buyer's actual pain in their language (see each campaign's
  `research.market.buyerLanguage`), not marketing abstractions.
- Short sentences over long ones. Concrete nouns over adjectives.
- Every claim needs a source (a real stat, a real case study) or it
  doesn't ship — see `approved-claims.md`.
