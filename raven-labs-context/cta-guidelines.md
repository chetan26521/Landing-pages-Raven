# Raven Labs — CTA Guidelines

- Prefer a low-friction, specific offer over a generic "Contact us":
  "Book my free [X] health check / assessment" outperforms "Get in touch"
  for cold PPC traffic.
- The CTA verb in the ad must match the CTA verb on the page exactly
  (message-match — checked by QA, see `lib/engine/qa.ts`).
- Every campaign needs a primary CTA and may have a secondary, lower-
  commitment CTA (e.g. "Download the guide") for visitors not ready to
  book.
