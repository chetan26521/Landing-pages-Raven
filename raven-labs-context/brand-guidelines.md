# Raven Labs — Brand Guidelines

Source of truth for visual identity, consumed by the design system in
`components/sections/` via the CSS tokens in `app/globals.css` and
`tailwind.config.ts`. Do not hard-code colors/fonts in a campaign — they
come from these tokens so every campaign stays visually consistent while
its composition (section choice, order, copy) stays campaign-specific.

## Colors
- Primary: `#4a00e1` (brand purple), with a 4-stop gradient through
  `#6400E1`, `#7900E1`, `#8300e2`.
- Neutrals: white/black plus a gray scale (50/100/200/400/600/800/900).

## Typography
- Display + body: Poppins (Quicksand as a fallback display face).
- Reports/emails only: Arial/Helvetica.
- Mobile body baseline is 16px to avoid iOS input zoom.

## Voice
- Direct, authoritative, no filler or throat-clearing.
- Australian English.
- Raven Labs and Fulqrom are always capitalised correctly.

## Non-negotiables
- Never invent client logos, testimonials, stats, awards, or partnerships.
- Never fabricate ABN/phone/email — these are legally required on Google
  Ads landing pages and must come from a human.
