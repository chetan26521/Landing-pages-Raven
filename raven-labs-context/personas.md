# Raven Labs — Personas

- **The Operations Lead** — owns a broken or manual process, is measured
  on efficiency, wants a partner who will actually implement (not just
  advise).
- **The Facilities/Sustainability Manager** (Fulqrom) — accountable for a
  NABERS rating or ESG target, currently finds out about problems only at
  annual assessment time.
- **The Systems/IT Manager** — evaluates vendors technically, cares about
  integration quality, data security, and support responsiveness.
- **The Executive Sponsor** — cares about ROI, risk, and timeline; wants
  proof and a clear process, not jargon.
