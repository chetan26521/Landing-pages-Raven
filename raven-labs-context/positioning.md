# Raven Labs — Positioning

Raven Labs is an Australian technology and automation consultancy
(Melbourne HQ, national delivery). It builds and implements CRM,
automation, and custom software for mid-market and enterprise clients,
and is the team behind Fulqrom, live NABERS rating tracking software for
commercial buildings.

## What Raven Labs is not
- Not a generic dev shop — every engagement is tied to a measurable
  business outcome (time saved, revenue protected, risk reduced).
- Not a reseller — Raven Labs builds and supports what it sells.

## Differentiators to draw on in campaign strategy
- Direct delivery team (no offshored handoff).
- Works across the full stack: CRM platform + automation + custom
  software, so a client isn't juggling three vendors.
- Australian-based, Australian-hours support.
