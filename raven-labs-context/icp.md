# Raven Labs — Ideal Customer Profile

- Mid-market and enterprise businesses in Australia (national), primarily
  B2B.
- Buyer roles: operations leaders, IT/systems managers, facilities and
  sustainability managers (Fulqrom), sales/RevOps leaders (CRM).
- Company stage: established businesses with existing systems to
  consolidate, migrate, or automate — not pre-revenue startups.
- Pain: manual processes, fragmented systems, annual/point-in-time
  reporting where continuous visibility would prevent costly surprises.
